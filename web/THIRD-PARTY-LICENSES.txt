Third-party licenses
====================

The demos' engine scripts, engine.v1.js (the Agent), engine.scope.v1.js,
engine.lab.v1.js, engine.ledger.v1.js and engine.exit.v1.js, hold VPN Works
engines compiled to WebAssembly with TinyGo. Each includes parts of the Go
standard library, the TinyGo runtime, TinyGo's loader (wasm_exec.js, derived
from the Go authors' file) and parts of the C library TinyGo links for
WebAssembly (wasi-libc, which includes code from musl). Their licenses
follow.
